# Contributing to Ouizja

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Ouizja! We welcome contributions from everyone.

## How to Contribute

1.  **Fork the repository** on GitHub.
2.  **Clone your fork** locally.
3.  **Create a new branch** for your feature or bug fix.
4.  **Make your changes**. Ensure your code follows the existing style and includes appropriate tests.
5.  **Run tests**:
    - For Rust changes: `cd simulation && cargo test`
    - For Kotlin changes: `./gradlew test`
6.  **Commit your changes** with clear and descriptive commit messages.
7.  **Push your branch** to your fork.
8.  **Open a Pull Request** against the main repository.
